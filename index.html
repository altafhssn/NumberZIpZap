Privacy Policy for ZipPath

Effective date: July 6, 2026
App: ZipPath (Android package: com.littletag.zipzap)
Publisher: Little Tag Art Studios

This Privacy Policy explains how the ZipPath mobile puzzle game processes information, why it is processed, when it is shared with service providers, and the choices available to you.

Summary of the service

ZipPath is a single-player number-path puzzle game with levels, a daily challenge, hints, locally saved progress, and optional online leaderboards. The game displays banner, interstitial, and rewarded advertisements supplied by Google AdMob. Google Play Games Services provides player authentication and leaderboards.

Little Tag Art Studios does not operate a separate game backend, sell personal information, or receive payment-card information. Game progress is stored in the app’s private storage on your device. Google processes limited information when Play Games or AdMob is used, as described below.

Information processed and why

1. Game progress stored on your device

ZipPath writes the following information to a local save file in the app’s private storage:

  • Levels unlocked and completed
  • Petals earned and completion times
  • Selected and unlocked color themes
  • In-game Drops balance
  • Daily Pond streak and last-completion date
  • Hint balance and last-refill time
  • Sound and haptic settings

The save file itself is not uploaded to Little Tag Art Studios or to Google. Some values derived from your progress—your petals and completion time—are submitted as a combined leaderboard score when you are signed in to Google Play Games.

2. Google Play Games Services

ZipPath attempts Google Play Games authentication when the app starts. If you have previously authorized Play Games for ZipPath, Google may sign you in automatically. Google manages the authentication flow and your Play Games account choices.

While you are signed in, ZipPath uses Play Games Services to submit and display leaderboard results. The app processes:

  • Your Play Games public display name
  • Your Play Games avatar image URL
  • Your leaderboard rank and numeric scores, which encode petals earned and completion time
  • Public display names, avatar image URLs, ranks, and scores of other players shown on the leaderboard

Little Tag Art Studios does not receive your Google account password, email address, or payment information through Play Games. Play Games data is shared with and processed by Google to authenticate players and provide leaderboards. You can manage your Play Games profile, privacy settings, and game data through your Google account and Play Games settings.

3. Google AdMob advertising

ZipPath uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK to display banner, interstitial, and rewarded ads. The SDK automatically collects and shares the following data with Google for advertising, analytics, and fraud-prevention purposes:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate general location
  • User interactions, including app launches, taps, and ad or video views
  • Diagnostic information, such as app/SDK performance, launch time, hangs, and energy usage
  • Device and account identifiers, including the Android Advertising ID, App Set ID, and, where applicable, other identifiers associated with accounts on the device
  • Basic device information such as device type, operating system, and locale

Google may use this information to provide, personalize, measure, and protect advertising. Little Tag Art Studios receives aggregated advertising reports, such as impressions and revenue, rather than your Google account credentials.

Where required by law, ZipPath uses Google’s User Messaging Platform to request and store your advertising consent choices. Depending on your region and choices, Google may serve personalized, non-personalized, or limited ads. You can also manage your Advertising ID and ad-privacy choices in Android or Google settings.

Learn more in Google’s Privacy Policy, Advertising Policies, and Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure.

4. Store crash reports and diagnostics

The app store or operating system may collect crash reports and technical diagnostics according to your device and store settings. Little Tag Art Studios may receive aggregated or pseudonymous diagnostic reports through the store’s developer console to identify and fix errors. ZipPath does not include a separate third-party analytics or attribution SDK.

Permissions

  • Internet: used for Google Play Games authentication and leaderboards, AdMob advertising, consent management, and related diagnostics.
  • Vibrate: used for optional haptic feedback during gameplay. Haptics can be disabled in the game’s settings.

ZipPath does not request access to precise device location, contacts, photos, calendar, microphone, or camera.

How information is shared

Little Tag Art Studios does not sell personal information. Information is shared only as needed with:

  • Google Play Games Services, for authentication and leaderboards
  • Google AdMob and the Google Mobile Ads SDK, for advertising, ad measurement, analytics, consent management, and fraud prevention
  • The app store and platform provider, for app distribution, security, crash reporting, and diagnostics

These providers process information under their own privacy policies and may process it in countries other than your own.

Security

Local progress is kept in the app’s private storage. Data sent to Google by the Google Mobile Ads SDK and Google Play Games Services is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, but we and our service providers use reasonable safeguards appropriate to the information processed.

Data retention and deletion

  • Local progress: remains on your device until you clear ZipPath’s app data or uninstall the app.
  • Play Games data: is retained by Google according to your Play Games settings and Google’s policies. You can manage or request deletion of Play Games data through your Google account or Play Games settings.
  • Advertising and diagnostic data: is retained by Google or the relevant app store according to that provider’s policies. You can manage ad privacy choices and reset or delete your Advertising ID in Android/Google settings.
  • Developer backend: Little Tag Art Studios does not maintain a separate backend copy of your progress or a separate ZipPath user account.

You may contact us for help with a privacy request. When information is held only by Google or an app store, we may direct you to that provider’s controls.

Children’s privacy

ZipPath is a general-audience game and is not directed to children under 13 or the minimum digital-consent age in their country. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children. Google Play Games, AdMob, and the app store process information under their own policies and account, age, consent, and regional settings. If you believe a child has provided personal information in connection with ZipPath, contact us so we can review the request and assist with the appropriate provider controls.

Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict certain processing of personal information, or to object to or withdraw consent for certain processing. Little Tag Art Studios does not sell personal information. To make a request, contact us below. We may need enough information to verify and respond to your request.
