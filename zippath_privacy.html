Privacy Policy for ZipPath

Effective date: July 3, 2026  ·  App: com.littletag.zipzap

This policy explains what information the ZipPath mobile game collects, how it is used, and the choices available to you. If any of this changes, we will update this page and revise the "Effective date" above.

Summary. ZipPath is a single-player puzzle game. We do not run our own servers, we do not sell your data, and we do not build advertising profiles. The only information the app processes is what is needed to save your progress on your device, to submit optional leaderboard scores through Google Play Games Services, and — when advertising is enabled — to show ads through Google AdMob. Everything you see and do inside the game stays on your device unless you explicitly opt in to Google Play Games.

What we collect and why

1. On-device save data (always)

The following is written to a local save file (user://zippath_save.json) inside the app's private storage on your device:

  • Which levels you have unlocked and completed
  • Petals earned per level and per pack
  • Palette (color theme) you have equipped and which palettes you have unlocked
  • Drops currency balance
  • Daily Pond streak and last completion date
  • Hint wallet and last refill time
  • Settings you have chosen (sound on/off, haptics on/off)

This file never leaves your device. Uninstalling ZipPath removes it.

2. Google Play Games Services (only if you sign in)

If you sign in to Google Play Games from within ZipPath, the following flows through Google's Play Games service so we can show you on leaderboards:

  • Your Google Play Games display name (a chosen gamer tag, not your real name unless you set it that way)
  • Your Play Games avatar image URL
  • The numeric score you submit to each leaderboard (a combined value of petals earned and time taken)

Google Play Games Services is provided by Google and governed by Google's Privacy Policy. ZipPath never sees your Google account email or your real name. If you do not sign in, no leaderboard data leaves the app.

3. Google AdMob (advertising, when enabled)

When advertising is active in ZipPath, ads are served by Google AdMob. To do this AdMob receives:

  • Your Android Advertising ID
  • Coarse device information (device model, OS version, general locale) that Google uses to select an ad
  • General ad performance signals

You can reset or delete your Advertising ID at any time via Android Settings → Privacy → Ads. You can opt out of personalized advertising there as well; ZipPath will continue to show non-personalized ads instead.

We do not receive individually identifying information from AdMob. We only see aggregate revenue and impression counts through the AdMob console.

4. Google Play Billing (only if you make a purchase)

If ZipPath offers in-app purchases (e.g. an ad-removal upgrade), those purchases are processed by Google Play Billing. ZipPath does not receive or store your payment information — Google handles the entire transaction. The app only receives a signal that the purchase completed so premium features can be unlocked on your device.

5. Crash reports and diagnostics

The Google Play Store may automatically collect crash reports and technical diagnostics from ZipPath in accordance with your Google Play settings. We receive only aggregated crash statistics through the Play Console. We do not run any additional third-party analytics, telemetry, or attribution SDK.

What we do NOT collect

  • Your name, email address, phone number, or postal address
  • Your location (coarse or fine)
  • Your contacts, photos, calendar, microphone, or camera
  • Your device identifiers other than the resettable Advertising ID used by AdMob
  • Any information about other apps installed on your device

Permissions the app requests

  • Internet — required to submit leaderboard scores, deliver ads, and process purchases. Not used to send your save data anywhere else.
  • Vibrate — used for gentle haptic feedback when you interact with the puzzle. Can be turned off in Settings.

Children's privacy

ZipPath is intended for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or under the age of digital consent in the applicable jurisdiction). Google Play Games Services and Google AdMob apply their own child-protection policies according to your device's age settings. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us in error, please contact us and we will delete anything associated with the account.

Data retention and deletion

  • Local save file: stays on your device until you uninstall the app or clear its data from Android Settings.
  • Leaderboard scores: retained by Google Play Games under Google's policies. To delete your Play Games profile or specific scores, use the Google Play Games app or the Play Games web dashboard.
  • AdMob signals: managed by Google. You can reset your Advertising ID or opt out of personalization via Android Settings.
  • We keep no separate backend record of your play, so there is nothing on our servers to delete.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights under laws such as the GDPR (EU), UK GDPR, or CCPA (California), including the right to access, correct, or delete personal information about you, and the right to opt out of the sale of personal information. ZipPath does not sell personal information. Because we do not maintain user accounts on our own servers, most requests can be resolved by adjusting your device settings, your Google Play Games profile, or by uninstalling the app.

If you have a request under one of these laws, contact us using the details below and we will respond within the timeframe required by the applicable law.

Third-party services referenced above

  • Google AdMob — Ads & Cookies
  • Google Play Billing — Privacy Policy
  • Google Play Store (distribution + crash reports)
